Evaluation

What the numbers mean

Each training-period user is scored against the full catalogue, with training-period items masked. A hit is an item they adopt in the test period but had not used before. Relevance is binary; the primary metric is NDCG@10, macro-averaged over users.

01

Leakage-free by construction

Mappings, catalogue, popularity, and model parameters are fit on the training period only. The test period is used once, for labels.

02

Baselines that can win

Popularity and item-kNN are first-class. A graph model earns its place only when it beats them on paired users.

03

Operable, not just runnable

Versioned artefacts, health checks, Prometheus counters, feedback capture, Docker, and a retraining runbook.
