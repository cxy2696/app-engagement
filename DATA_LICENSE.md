## Permitted use (research reproducibility)

You may download and process the files to reproduce the benchmark described in this repository, provided that you:

1. do not attempt to re-identify any panelist;
2. do not redistribute the files or derived row-level data;
3. do not use the files for advertising, eligibility, credit, insurance, employment, or surveillance decisions;
4. cite this repository and, when known, the original data provider.

## Prohibited use

- Re-identification or linkage with external datasets.
- Publication of per-panelist records or profiles.
- Commercial redistribution or incorporation into a data product.
- Any use that violates applicable privacy or data-protection law.

## Derived artefacts

Aggregate metrics, model parameters, and documentation produced by this repository may be redistributed under the code licence, provided they do not permit reconstruction of row-level records. Model artefacts contain item factors and user factors; treat them as potentially sensitive and do not publish artefacts trained on data you are not permitted to share.
